About Me

Nurse & Educator with Heart

Dekla Aluka is a BScN-qualified registered nurse from Nairobi, Kenya. She delivers compassionate care across all ages — from tiny newborns to beloved elders — with warmth, skill, and a whole lot of heart.

Known for her smile, enthusiasm, and genuine love for teaching, Dekla believes healthcare should feel kind, never scary. She's the nurse who holds your hand, answers every question, and makes sure you leave feeling informed and cared for.

Whether she's giving a health talk, running a wellness consultation, or mentoring the next generation of nurses, Dekla brings energy, empathy, and excellence to everything she does.

❤ My Why

Born & Raised in Nairobi

Growing up in Nairobi, Dekla saw how quality healthcare and health education could change lives. Her passion for teaching and helping others led her straight to nursing — the perfect blend of science and heart.

She's the nurse who loves to teach, share knowledge, and inspire young minds. Whether in a hospital, a classroom, or a community hall, she brings genuine warmth and a playful spirit to her work.

👶 🤱 👵 ❤️
I became a nurse because I wanted a career that combines science and heart — the ability to heal, to teach, and to inspire. Every patient is a story, and I want to be a positive chapter in theirs.
— Dekla Aluka, BScN
